The grounds, the schedule, the rules, and how the whole thing works.</p> </header> <!-- MAP --> <section class="section light"> <div class="section-inner"> <h2>The Grounds</h2> <p class="lead">Bone's Kayak &amp; Campground.<br>Address: 16520 Bakerville Rd, Hurricane Mills, TN 37078.<br>Camp, dance floor, and river all within a short wander.</p> <p class="note">An official festival map is on the way.</p> </div> </section> <!-- SCHEDULE --> <section class="section dark"> <div class="section-inner"> <h2>Schedule</h2> <p class="lead">Coming soon.</p> </div> </section> <!-- KNOW BEFORE YOU GO --> <section class="section light"> <div class="section-inner"> <span class="eyebrow" style="color:var(--amber)">Know before you go</span> <h2>FAQ &amp; Rules</h2> <div class="faq-search"> <label for="faq-q">Search the FAQ</label> <input id="faq-q" type="search" autocomplete="off" placeholder="Type what you're wondering: kayaks, dogs, dinner, parking..."> <p class="faq-count" id="faq-count" role="status" aria-live="polite"></p> </div> <div class="faq" id="faq"> <h3 class="faq-cat">Getting here &amp; checking in</h3> <details data-keys="arrival arrive gates open entry entrance early friday saturday what time get there late arrival"> <summary>When can I arrive?</summary> <p>Gates open Friday at 3pm. If Friday doesn't work, come Saturday morning instead. Either way, your first stop is the check-in tent on the gravel drive by the entrance, before you park.</p> </details> <details data-keys="check in checkin ticket qr code scan waiver will call merch shirt t-shirt tee registration"> <summary>What happens at check-in?</summary> <p>You show your ticket QR code, we scan it, and everyone in the car signs a waiver. We'll hand you a printed guide to the weekend, point you at the kayak info, and send you on to parking. Festival shirts are for sale right there at the tent, and it's easier to grab one before you set up camp.</p> </details> <details data-keys="parking park camp camping campsite tent setup rv car walk distance how far"> <summary>Where do I park, and where do I camp?</summary> <p>Parking is on site, and you camp in front of your vehicle in the spot you're given. It's about a five minute walk downhill from camping to the main grounds and the dance floor, so bring a light for after dark.</p> </details> <details data-keys="cell service signal phone reception wifi internet map guide one pager paper printed"> <summary>Will I have cell service? Is there a paper guide?</summary> <p>Cell service out here is a rumor. Everyone gets a printed one-pager at check-in with the map, the schedule, kayak times, when the music stops, when to be out Sunday, and who to call in an emergency. Keep it in your pocket.</p> </details> <h3 class="faq-cat">Schedule &amp; music</h3> <details data-keys="schedule itinerary agenda timeline plan friday saturday sunday what happens overview"> <summary>What does the weekend actually look like?</summary> <p>Friday: gates at 3pm, music on the small stage up top from 6pm, dinner at 6, field games in the evening, music ends at midnight, movie after. Saturday: kayak shuttles and the float during the day, dinner at the mess hall, then music at the main stage down below until 3am. Sunday: pack up and head out by 11am.</p> </details> <details data-keys="music end curfew late night last set closing time quiet hours"> <summary>What time does the music end each night?</summary> <p>Friday the music ends at midnight. Saturday it runs until 3am.</p> </details> <details data-keys="leave sunday checkout check out departure exit pack up end of festival"> <summary>When do I have to leave on Sunday?</summary> <p>Everybody out by 11am Sunday. Pack it up, take it with you, and leave your spot cleaner than you found it.</p> </details> <details data-keys="set times dj times stage times lineup schedule who plays when"> <summary>Where do I find set times?</summary> <p>Set times get posted at the check-in tent during the weekend, and they'll land on the lineup page here once they're locked.</p> </details> <details data-keys="field games field day competition tug of war relay teams raccoon frog turtle butterfly activities"> <summary>What are the field games?</summary> <p>Friday evening on the field up top. You'll be sorted onto a critter team, raccoon, frog, turtle or butterfly, and then it's tug of war, relays, and general nonsense. You have a team whether you asked for one or not.</p> </details> <details data-keys="movie film screening late night friday after hours"> <summary>Is there a movie?</summary> <p>Yes. Friday night after the music ends, up top.</p> </details> <h3 class="faq-cat">The river &amp; kayaks</h3> <details data-keys="float river kayak canoe shuttle drop in how long paddle saturday water time"> <summary>How does the float work?</summary> <p>The shuttle to the drop-in runs Saturday from 9am to noon. Once you're on the water, the float back to the festival takes two to four hours, and the float window closes at 4pm. Miss the shuttle, miss the river, so don't sleep in.</p> </details> <details data-keys="rent rental kayak canoe boat reservation booking bones link gear paddle life jacket"> <summary>How do I rent a kayak or canoe?</summary> <p>All boat reservations go through <a href="https://www.bonescanoeandcampground.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="color:var(--amber)">Bone's Kayak &amp; Campground</a>, not River Dance. You'll get a rental link once you buy your River Dance ticket. Rentals, gear, and rental questions are all Bone's, we just point.</p> </details> <details data-keys="swim swimming water river safety dip"> <summary>Can I swim in the river?</summary> <p>You can swim during the float. Once you're back ashore, the swimming is done. No getting back in the water after the float.</p> </details> <h3 class="faq-cat">Food, drink &amp; water</h3> <details data-keys="food eat dinner meal vendor included vegan vegetarian gluten free breakfast lunch snacks kitchen mess hall"> <summary>What's the deal with food?</summary> <p>Dinner Saturday evening is included with your ticket, served at the mess hall down below. Friday there's a food vendor on site up top with food for purchase starting at 6pm. Gluten-free, vegetarian, and vegan options both nights. Pack some snacks for the gaps and for the river.</p> </details> <details data-keys="water drinking water hydration refill bottle station potable thirsty heat"> <summary>Is there drinking water?</summary> <p>Free water stations are set up on the grounds, up top and down below. Bring a refillable bottle. It's August in a river valley, so drink more than you think you need.</p> </details> <details data-keys="bar drinks alcohol beer cocktail booze buy drink pay"> <summary>Is there a bar?</summary> <p>Yes. Friday the bar is up top, and Friday food and drink both order at the bar. Saturday the bar moves down below with the rest of the festival and opens in the afternoon.</p> </details> <details data-keys="byob own drinks alcohol cooler glass bottles cans containers"> <summary>Can I bring my own drinks? What about glass?</summary> <p>Plastic and metal containers are fine. No glass at River Dance, period.</p> </details> <h3 class="faq-cat">Rules &amp; the grounds</h3> <details data-keys="age 21 minors kids children id age limit adults only"> <summary>Is River Dance 21+?</summary> <p>Yes. River Dance is a 21 and up event.</p> </details> <details data-keys="pet dog cat animal service animal bring dog"> <summary>Can I bring my pet?</summary> <p>No pets. Please leave your furry friends at home.</p> </details> <details data-keys="boundaries fence grounds off limits where can i go property trespass area"> <summary>Where can I go on the grounds?</summary> <p>Stay inside the clearly marked, fenced-in festival area at all times. Anything outside the fence is off-limits. If we catch you out of bounds, we will call your boss and tell them what you're really doing.</p> </details> <details data-keys="bring pack packing list what to bring supplies gear checklist costume flashlight"> <summary>What should I bring?</summary> <p>Tent and sleeping setup, river shoes, sun protection, a flashlight or headlamp, a refillable water bottle, snacks, and a costume or three.</p> </details> <details data-keys="bathroom bathrooms restroom toilet porta potty porta john facilities"> <summary>Where are the bathrooms?</summary> <p>There are permanent bathrooms up top, plus porta johns spread around the grounds.</p> </details> <h3 class="faq-cat">Help &amp; safety</h3> <details data-keys="lost found missing phone keys wallet sunglasses lost and found stolen"> <summary>I lost something. Or found something.</summary> <p>The check-in tent is the one and only lost and found. If you find something that isn't yours, walk it up there. If you lost your phone, your keys, or your sunglasses, go check.</p> </details> <details data-keys="help emergency medical emt first aid injury hospital 911 safety security problem sick"> <summary>What if I need help or something goes wrong?</summary> <p>For an emergency, call 911. On site, head to the check-in tent or flag down anyone with a walkie. There's an EMT stationed at the check-in tent on Saturday. Non-emergency and hospital numbers are printed on the one-pager you get at check-in.</p> </details> <details data-keys="weather rain storm heat hot refund cancel cancelled forecast shade sunscreen"> <summary>What about weather?</summary> <p>It's a river valley in Tennessee in August. Plan for heat, pop-up rain, and mud. River Dance happens rain or shine. If we have to cancel for extreme weather, tickets are refunded at point of purchase.</p> </details> <p class="faq-empty">Nothing matches that one.
